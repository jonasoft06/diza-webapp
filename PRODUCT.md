# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, both tied to physical books used in a classroom setting:

- **Students** using the platform alongside a physical book to get help understanding its content.
- **Teachers** using the platform to build activities and class material based on the physical book.

## Product Purpose

Diza supports the use of physical books by pairing them with an AI academic tutor. Students get their doubts about the book's content resolved by the tutor; teachers get help creating activities and material for class based on the book.

## Positioning

Diza's mechanism is an AI tutor scoped to a specific physical book — not a general-purpose chatbot or a digital-book replacement. It exists to extend and support the physical book, not substitute it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI tutor answers student questions grounded in the content of a specific physical book.
- AI-assisted generation of classroom activities and material for teachers, based on the book.
- Undecided: how books are linked/registered to the platform, whether there is a paid tier, and whether the platform serves individual schools or is sold direct to students/teachers.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Diza".
- Existing brand palette (vino, dorado, papel, tinta tones) and typography (Playfair Display + Karla) are confirmed and binding.

## Evidence on Hand

No real catalog of books, testimonials, or case studies yet. Do not fabricate school names, user counts, or example book titles — treat any such content in future work as placeholder unless the user provides real evidence.

## Product Principles

- The physical book is the anchor; the platform's job is to support it, not replace it.
- Answers and generated material must stay grounded in the specific book in use, not generic content.
- Serve two distinct jobs (student doubt-solving, teacher material creation) without blurring them into one generic "AI assistant" experience.
